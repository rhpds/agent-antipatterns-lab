# SB-2024-117 K-400 coolant pump mechanical seal service

## Applies to
K-400 chillers fitted with coolant pump assembly KI-CP-40817 and seal kit KI-SK-40233.

## Service interval
Replace the coolant pump mechanical seal every 6,500 operating hours or 14 months, whichever comes first. Units in dusty environments, or running coolant glycol above 35 percent, should be serviced at 4,500 operating hours.

## Inspection
At each quarterly visit, check the weep port beneath the pump housing. Any steady drip means the seal has failed early and must be replaced before the unit is returned to service.
