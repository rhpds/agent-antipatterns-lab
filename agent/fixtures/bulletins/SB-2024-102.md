# SB-2024-102 K-400 coolant pressure fault F-21

## Applies to
K-400 chillers, controller firmware 2.3 and later.

## Symptom
Controller raises fault F-21 (coolant loop pressure low) during start-up or under load.

## Action
Bleed air from the coolant loop at the high point vent, then confirm loop pressure reaches 2.1 bar within 90 seconds. If the fault returns, inspect the circulation pump assembly and its mechanical seal.
