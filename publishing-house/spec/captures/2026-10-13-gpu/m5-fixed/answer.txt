Question: What's the recommended service interval for the K-400 coolant pump seal?
Endpoint: http://qwen3-8b-b-predictor.llm-serving.svc:8080/v1

Answer:


The recommended service interval for the K-400 coolant pump seal is **every 6,500 operating hours or 14 months**, whichever comes first. However, units in **dusty environments** or those running coolant glycol above **35%** should be serviced at **4,500 hours** instead. 

**Key details from bulletin SB-2024-117**:
- Replace seal kit **KI-SK-40233** with pump assembly **KI-CP-40817**.
- **Inspect the weep port** during quarterly visits; a steady drip indicates seal failure requiring immediate replacement.

Always reference the full bulletin for context.
